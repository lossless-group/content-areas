# Asetek

## Retrieved sources

https://www.asetek.com/ [^E1] 

Asetek is a Denmark-based company founded in 2000 that designs, manufactures, and sells liquid-cooling hardware for high-performance computing, data centers, and immersive gaming. It reports 131 employees and lists customers or partners including Acer, ASUS, CRAY, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Intel, and AMD. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

Asetek’s core proposition is to remove heat from high-performance computing systems more efficiently through liquid-cooling technology, while also supplying immersive gaming hardware through its SimSports business. [^E1]

Its products serve two principal areas: liquid cooling for servers, data centers, and AI-oriented computing, and gaming or sim-racing hardware designed around high-performance interaction and immersion. [^E1]

- **[[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Concepts/Liquid Cooling Systems|Liquid Cooling]] systems** for high-performance computing and data-center applications. [^E1]
- **Cooling technologies for AI and computing workloads** used by [[Original Equipment Manufacturers|OEMs]] and technology partners. [^E1]
- **Gaming hardware** supporting immersive gaming experiences. [^E1]
- **Sim-racing wheelbases, pedals, and steering hardware** sold under the Asetek SimSports brand. [1]
- **RaceHub software and device-management tooling** for configuring compatible sim-racing hardware. [1]
- **OEM and partner integration** with major computer and server manufacturers. [^E1]

Asetek fits naturally within [[concepts/Market-Categories/Datacenter Operations|Datacenter Operations]] because its cooling systems address thermal management for servers and high-density computing environments.

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Asetek is for OEMs, server and data-center operators, and other technology partners that need liquid cooling for high-performance or AI-related computing, as well as consumers seeking dedicated sim-racing hardware. [^E1] [1]

It is not primarily for buyers seeking general-purpose office PCs, basic air-cooling accessories, or software-only AI infrastructure.

### Viable Alternatives

- **CoolIT Systems** — liquid-cooling infrastructure for data centers and high-performance computing.

- **Vertiv** — broader data-center power and cooling infrastructure.
