## Value Proposition & Features

Oracle Cloud Infrastructure is Oracle’s public-cloud platform for compute, storage, networking, databases, analytics, security, and AI workloads. Oracle positions OCI as an integrated platform that combines cloud infrastructure with Oracle databases and enterprise applications. [^E1]

Its strongest differentiator is support for large AI and GPU deployments alongside enterprise database workloads. Oracle’s broader cloud stack also includes autonomous database capabilities, integrated applications, and networking services such as Oracle Dyn. [^E1] [^E2]

- **AI and GPU infrastructure:** OCI’s infrastructure revenue increased 121% year over year to $7.4 billion in Oracle’s fiscal Q1 2027. [^zhvo7h]
- **Large-scale data centers:** Oracle added 850 megawatts of data-center capacity during that quarter. [^mr0fg7]
- **Compute and storage:** OCI provides the foundational infrastructure used to run applications, databases, and machine-learning workloads. [^E1]
- **Database services:** OCI supports Oracle database workloads and multicloud database deployments.
- **Multicloud connectivity:** Oracle reported 70 multicloud database regions and 119 availability zones after planned Azure and AWS expansion. [^6kgcix]
- **Networking and security:** Oracle Dyn provides DNS, web-application security, and email-delivery services that can operate with OCI. [^E2]
- **Enterprise integration:** OCI is part of Oracle’s integrated stack spanning infrastructure, databases, AI data services, and applications. [^E1]
- **AI capacity utilization:** Oracle reported 97.9% utilization for its AI infrastructure and an average 20% premium on renewed or resold GPUs. [^28fd0c]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

OCI is aimed at enterprises running Oracle databases and applications, organizations requiring large-scale GPU or AI capacity, and customers seeking private connectivity across multiple clouds. Its multicloud database regions and hyperscaler interconnects are particularly relevant to enterprises that distribute workloads across Oracle, Microsoft Azure, and Amazon Web Services. [^6kgcix]

OCI is less suited to small users seeking the simplest developer experience, purely serverless applications with minimal infrastructure management, or customers that do not need Oracle database integration, enterprise support, or large-scale compute capacity.

## Viable Alternatives

- **[[Tooling/Software Development/Cloud Infrastructure/Amazon Web Services|Amazon Web Services]]:** Broad public-cloud service coverage and a large ecosystem, making it a general-purpose alternative to OCI.
- **[[Tooling/Software Development/Cloud Infrastructure/Azure|Microsoft Azure]]:** Strong enterprise and Microsoft-stack integration, plus multicloud database cooperation with Oracle.
- **[[Tooling/Software Development/Cloud Infrastructure/Google Cloud|Google Cloud]]:** Competitive AI, data, and machine-learning infrastructure.
- **[[IBM Cloud]]:** Enterprise, regulated-industry, and hybrid-cloud alternative.
- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/AI Infrastructure/CoreWeave|CoreWeave]]:** Specialized GPU-cloud alternative for AI training and inference.
