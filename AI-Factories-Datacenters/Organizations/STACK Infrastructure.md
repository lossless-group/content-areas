### STACK Infrastructure —
https://stackinfra.com/ [^E1] 

STACK Infrastructure provides campus, build-to-suit, colocation, and powered-shell data-center facilities and services across the Americas, EMEA, and APAC. [^E1] Its headquarters are in Denver, Colorado, and the company was founded in 2019. [^E1]

# Value Proposition & Features

STACK Infrastructure positions itself as a digital-infrastructure partner for companies requiring scalable data-center capacity, particularly hyperscalers and other large technology businesses. [^E1]

Its offering combines development, construction, ownership, and operation of data-center campuses, enabling customers to obtain purpose-built capacity or colocation space across multiple global regions. [^E1]

- **Build-to-suit capacity:** Custom facilities developed for a customer’s technical, power, and deployment requirements. [^E1]
- **Hyperscale campuses:** Large-scale sites designed to support substantial computing deployments. [^E1]
- **Colocation:** Third-party data-center space and associated infrastructure for customers that do not own the facility. [^E1]
- **Powered shell facilities:** Data-center buildings with core infrastructure and power availability, allowing customers to complete deployment fit-out. [^E1]
- **Campus development:** Multi-building or multi-phase sites intended for long-term capacity expansion. [^E1]
- **Global delivery footprint:** Operations and development across the Americas, EMEA, and APAC. [^E1]

# History and Origin Story

STACK launched in 2019 by combining existing operating data centers in six U.S. markets with development parcels in five markets. [^chj456] Its initial offering included hyperscale campuses, wholesale colocation, and powered-shell facilities, and the business was sponsored by IPI Partners. [^chj456] STACK’s current profile describes it as a global developer and operator serving the Americas, EMEA, and APAC. [^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

STACK is aimed at [[Hyperscale Cloud Providers|Hyperscalers]], large technology companies, and enterprises needing substantial, scalable data-center capacity through [[concepts/Build-to-Suit Development|Build-to-Suit]], campus, [[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Concepts/Colocation Services|Colocation]], or powered-shell arrangements. [^E1]

It is not positioned as a self-service cloud platform, a small-business hosting provider, or a software product with publicly listed subscription tiers; its offering is physical infrastructure and related facility services. [^E1]
