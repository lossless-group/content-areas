# Value Proposition & Features

Digital Realty connects enterprises, cloud providers, networks, and data communities through colocation, interconnection, and hyperscale infrastructure. Its PlatformDIGITAL proposition is built around secure data collaboration and managing “Data Gravity” challenges. [^9x13nk] [^E1]

Its core architecture combines physical data centers with connectivity and programmable interconnection services. PlatformDIGITAL uses the company’s “Pervasive Datacenter Architecture” methodology to support cloud transformation and AI workloads. [^9x13nk]

Priority features:

- **[[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Concepts/Colocation Services|Colocation]]:** Data-center space and infrastructure for enterprise, cloud, and service-provider workloads. [^E1]
- **Interconnection:** Cross-connects, ServiceFabric, and bulk-fiber connectivity for linking customers, clouds, and networks. [^v5k8h1]
- **[[Hyperscale Cloud Providers|Hyperscalers]] capacity:** Large data-center deployments for cloud and AI customers. [^v5k8h1]
- **PlatformDIGITAL:** A global platform for secure data collaboration and distributed infrastructure. [^9x13nk] [^E1]
- **[[AI-Ready Infrastructure]]:** Facilities and architecture designed to support emerging AI workloads. [^9x13nk]
- **Programmable control:** [[ServiceFabric]] [[concepts/Explainers for AI/Model Context Protocol|MCP]] provides [[Vocabulary/AI Native Applications|AI-Native]] programmable control across more than 800 data centers. [^gxu2y2]
- **Global footprint:** A worldwide data-center portfolio serving multiple major digital-infrastructure markets. [^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Digital Realty is for enterprises, cloud providers, hyperscalers, carriers, and service providers that need colocation, interconnection, hyperscale capacity, or geographically distributed digital infrastructure. [^E1] [^v5k8h1] Its large-footprint model is particularly suited to customers with sustained capacity, connectivity, compliance, or data-collaboration requirements.

It is not a low-cost, self-service hosting product for small workloads that need only a virtual machine or basic cloud storage. Customers seeking standardized infrastructure without facility, network, or compliance requirements would generally fit a public-cloud or managed-hosting provider better than a global data-center operator.
