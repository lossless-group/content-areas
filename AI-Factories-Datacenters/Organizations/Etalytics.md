Etalytics is a Darmstadt, Germany–based industrial-energy software company founded in 2020. Its etaONE platform combines real-time operational data, physics-based digital twins, predictive analytics, and automated optimization to improve cooling, power, and utility-system efficiency without replacing existing infrastructure. [^E1] [^hy2jjt]

## Value Proposition & Features

Etalytics targets operators of data centers and energy-intensive industrial sites that need lower energy consumption, improved resilience, and continued operator control. Its software optimizes existing cooling, HVAC, and energy infrastructure within predefined technical limits. [^E1] [^hy2jjt]

Core features:

- **Physics-based [[concepts/Market-Categories/Digital Twins|Digital Twins]]:** [[concepts/Explainers for AI/Physics-Based Models|Physics-Based Models]] and equipment, system topology, operating relationships, load, environmental conditions, and expected system behavior. [^hy2jjt]
- **Predictive analytics:** Uses operational data and equipment behavior to assess current performance and anticipate more efficient operating points. [^hy2jjt]
- **Automated setpoint optimization:** Calculates and writes optimized control settings back to the building-management system; one reported deployment recalculates settings every two to three minutes. [^fzxod6]
- **Cooling optimization:** Optimizes variables such as cooling-tower outlet temperature and recooling-pump flow. [^x6dy5n]
- **Real-time operational intelligence:** Combines live telemetry with physics-based models to support mission-critical energy infrastructure. [^hy2jjt]
- **Existing-infrastructure integration:** Operates on top of a site’s building-management system rather than requiring replacement of the cooling plant. [^fzxod6]
- **Operator controls and limits:** Maintains predefined operating boundaries and operator control during automated optimization. [^x6dy5n]
- **etaONE IQ:** Etalytics announced an AI assistant intended to provide reasoning and proactive agents for energy operations. [^6huqrc] [^fzxod6]

# History and Origin Story

Etalytics was founded in 2020 as a spin-off of TU Darmstadt and grew from research combining mechanical engineering, computer science, and energy science. Niklas Panten co-founded the company and became chief executive, while Thomas Weber became chief strategy officer and Björn König became chief technology officer; the founding team formed in 2019. [^hy2jjt] [^fzxod6]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Etalytics is designed for data-center operators and energy-intensive industrial organizations—such as automotive, pharmaceutical, chemical, and other mission-critical facilities—that have complex cooling, power, HVAC, or utility systems and usable operational telemetry. [^hy2jjt]

It is not positioned for small sites without complex energy infrastructure, sufficient instrumentation, or a building-management/control system capable of receiving optimized setpoints; this is an inference from the platform’s documented operating model. [^fzxod6] [^x6dy5n]

### Viable Alternatives

- **[[EkkoSense]]:** A data-center optimization platform focused on understanding conditions inside the data hall; reporting contrasts it with Etalytics’ simulation of the cooling system. [^p5zb5a]
- **In-house building-management-system optimization:** A lower-cost alternative for operators with internal controls engineering, though it may lack Etalytics’ combined digital-twin and predictive-optimization layer. [^fzxod6] [^x6dy5n]
- **Conventional controls engineering:** Rule-based tuning and manual commissioning can improve cooling performance without deploying an AI optimization platform.
- **Energy-management software:** Broader industrial energy-management systems may provide monitoring and load management but may not offer Etalytics’ physics-based cooling optimization.
