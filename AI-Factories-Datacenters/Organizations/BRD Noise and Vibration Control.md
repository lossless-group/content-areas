BRD HUSHCORE® is a line of HVAC noise and vibration control products designed for acoustic design and remediation in mechanical/HVAC installations. The company provides consultative analysis, design services, and manufacturing of sound/vibration treatments to meet project-driven acceptance criteria and reduce equipment noise. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

BRD HUSHCORE® combines consultative acoustic analysis, treatment design, and manufacturing for [[concepts/Market-Categories/Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning|HVAC]] and mechanical installations. Its stated value is reducing equipment noise and vibration while helping projects meet specified acceptance criteria. [^E1]

- HVAC noise-control products for mechanical installations. [^E1]
- Vibration-control treatments for HVAC equipment. [^E1]
- Acoustic analysis and consultation. [^E1]
- Project-specific treatment design. [^E1]
- Manufacturing of sound and vibration treatments. [^E1]
- Remediation support for existing mechanical installations. [^E1]
