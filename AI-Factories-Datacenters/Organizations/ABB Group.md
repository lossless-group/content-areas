# Value Proposition & Features

ABB Group is a global technology company focused on **electrification and automation**, helping industrial, utility, transport, and infrastructure customers improve performance while reducing resource use. [^z56wep] Its digital portfolio, ABB Ability, connects industrial expertise and technologies from device level through edge and cloud services. [^E1]

Core features:

- **Electrification:** Products and systems for electrical distribution, power management, and industrial energy efficiency. [^z56wep]
- **Automation:** Industrial control, instrumentation, communications, and process-automation technologies. [^8xauv1]
- **Motion:** Technologies for motors, drives, and motion-control applications across industrial sectors. [^pp7jak]
- **Digitalization:** ABB Ability combines connected equipment, analytics, monitoring, optimization software, and digital services across the device-to-cloud stack. [^E1]
- **Robotics and machine automation:** ABB Ventures identifies robotics and industrial AI as strategic investment areas, while ABB’s operating businesses provide automation technologies for industrial customers. [^E2]
- **E-mobility:** Charging infrastructure for public, private, roadside, destination, and commercial applications. [^E3]
- **Smart buildings:** Integrated building systems covering HVAC, lighting, occupancy, energy, and security, including ABB Ability BuildingPro. [^E4]
- **Direct-current infrastructure:** ABB announced Infinitus, a DC portfolio aimed at managing the rising power requirements of AI data centers. [^mzmy04]

# History and Origin Story

ABB’s modern corporate structure was created in 1988 through the merger of Sweden’s ASEA and Switzerland’s Brown, Boveri & Cie.; the company’s industrial heritage extends back more than 140 years. [^pp7jak] ABB’s current focus is organized around electrification, motion, automation, and the ABB Ability digital portfolio. [^z56wep] [^pp7jak]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

ABB is primarily for industrial, utility, transport, infrastructure, building, and commercial customers that need integrated electrification, automation, motion, charging, or industrial-digitalization systems. [^z56wep] [^wqqr3l]

It is not primarily for individual consumers seeking standalone household software or low-complexity plug-and-play equipment; ABB’s offerings are generally engineered, configured, installed, and serviced for organizational deployments. [^E1] [^E3] [^E4]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Siemens:** Broad competing portfolio across electrification, industrial automation, drives, building systems, and digital industrial technologies.
- **Schneider Electric:** Competes in electrical distribution, energy management, industrial automation, and building-management systems.
- **Rockwell Automation:** Competes particularly in industrial automation, control, manufacturing software, and connected operations.
- **Honeywell:** Competes in industrial control, process automation, building technologies, and aerospace-related systems.
- **Emerson:** Competes in process automation, instrumentation, industrial software, and control systems.
