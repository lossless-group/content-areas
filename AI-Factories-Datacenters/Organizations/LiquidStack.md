## Value Proposition & Features

LiquidStack is a **Trane Technologies business** focused on liquid-cooling infrastructure for high-density AI and data-center deployments. [^hw09xx] Its value proposition is to provide scalable, architecture-agnostic cooling that can accommodate changing GPU platforms and rising rack densities without repeated facility redesigns. [^hw09xx] [^6o2tx9]

Core products include **coolant distribution units (CDUs)** that circulate liquid between facility cooling systems and server-side direct-to-chip cold plates. [^hw09xx] [^x5w4fy] The portfolio includes the **GigaModular CDU**, a centralized platform with capacity expanded to 14 MW, and the newer **CDU 2.X**, which supports end-of-row and rack-adjacent deployments. [^6o2tx9] [^qdhao3]

Priority features:

- Architecture-agnostic support for multiple current and emerging AI processor platforms. [^hw09xx] [^6o2tx9]
- CDU 2.X flow capability of up to **3,750 liters per minute at 3.5 bar**. [^hw09xx] [^6o2tx9]
- Support for facility inlet temperatures up to **45°C**. [^hw09xx] [^7cikj0]
- End-of-row and rack-adjacent installation configurations. [^hw09xx] [^6o2tx9]
- Configurable control valves, power feeds, and redundancy options. [^hw09xx] [^6o2tx9]
- Dual-feed A/B power configurations with automatic transfer-switch support. [^hw09xx] [^7cikj0]
- GigaModular capacity up to **14 MW**. [^6o2tx9] [^qdhao3]
- NVIDIA DSX Ready qualification for the GigaModular CDU. [^qdhao3]

LiquidStack’s facility-level approach contrasts with [[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Organizations/Corintis|Corintis]], which develops processor-level microfluidic cooling rather than CDU and facility-distribution systems.

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

LiquidStack is aimed at data-center operators, AI infrastructure providers, OEMs, and engineering teams deploying high-density GPU systems that require liquid cooling and scalable facility-side distribution. [^hw09xx] [^6o2tx9] Its CDU products are particularly relevant where operators need flexible placement, redundancy, high flow rates, and support for changing processor architectures. [^hw09xx] [^7cikj0]

It is not a conventional software product, consumer cooling product, or low-density server-room solution; the available evidence positions it as specialized thermal infrastructure for enterprise and hyperscale AI deployments. [^hw09xx] [^6o2tx9]
