## Value Proposition & Features

Vantage Data Centers designs, develops, and operates large-scale wholesale data-center campuses for hyperscalers, cloud providers, and large enterprises. Its proposition centers on delivering scalable capacity for AI and cloud workloads across multiple global markets. [^E1] [^xyp4nn]

Core features include [[Rack-Ready]] and whitespace capacity, [[concepts/Build-to-Suit Development]] development, and campuses designed for high-density AI infrastructure. [^xyp4nn]

Priority features:

- **Hyperscale campuses** across five continents and 26 markets. [^xyp4nn]
- **Build-to-suit capacity** for large cloud and AI customers. [^xyp4nn]
- **Rack-ready and whitespace solutions** for customer deployment. [^xyp4nn]
- **High-density computing** supporting liquid-cooled racks exceeding 250 kW in planned AI facilities. [^us3u7s]
- **Global expansion platform** spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and Asia-Pacific. [^ck8p56]
- **Flexible financing and development capacity**, including revolving credit and asset-backed financing. [^uo57mo]
- **Campus-scale power availability**, with a reported planned portfolio exceeding 9 GW of IT capacity. [^xyp4nn]

# History and Origin Story

Vantage Data Centers was founded in 2010 and is headquartered in Denver, Colorado. [^E1] Its major recent inflection point has been rapid expansion of AI-oriented hyperscale infrastructure, including the Texas Frontier campus and the Wisconsin project connected with Oracle and OpenAI’s Stargate initiative. ## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Vantage is aimed at hyperscalers, cloud providers, AI infrastructure companies, and large enterprises requiring campus-scale power, customized deployments, or wholesale data-center capacity. [^E1] [^xyp4nn]

It is not positioned as a consumer colocation provider or a small-business hosting service; the retrieved material describes large-scale campuses, institutional financing, and multi-gigawatt development programs. [^us3u7s] [^xyp4nn]

### Viable Alternatives

- **STACK Infrastructure:** A competing hyperscale data-center developer and operator serving large cloud and enterprise workloads.
- **QTS Data Centers:** A large-scale data-center platform offering hyperscale and enterprise capacity.

- **Equinix:** A major global colocation and interconnection provider, particularly strong for network-dense deployments.
