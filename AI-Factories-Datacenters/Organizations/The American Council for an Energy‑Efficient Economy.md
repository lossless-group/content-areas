# The American Council for an Energy‑Efficient Economy

- ![ACEEE logo or official homepage hero image](https://www.aceee.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/share-image.png)
- _ACEEE is an independent nonprofit research and policy organization that turns energy-efficiency research into practical programs and public policy._
- ACEEE is a **nonprofit research organization** focused on reducing energy waste, lowering energy costs, addressing climate pollution, and supporting a more equitable energy system. [^bi5auw] [^j4ffdw] It works nationally across buildings, transportation, industry, and the power sector, and has operated since 1980. [^wx5w41] Consultants track ACEEE because its research, rankings, and policy analysis can influence energy programs, regulation, and investment decisions.

## Mission and Identity

> “ACEEE advances solutions that lower bills, cut energy waste and climate pollution, and build a more reliable, equitable energy system for all.” [^wx5w41]

ACEEE positions itself as an independent source of rigorous research, policy expertise, and technical assistance for policymakers, utilities, community organizations, businesses, households, and other actors working to improve energy use. [^bi5auw] [^wx5w41] It emphasizes practical implementation alongside analysis, including investments, programs, and behavior changes that use energy more effectively. [^bi5auw]

- **Operating principles:** ACEEE describes its work as independent analysis grounded in research, policy expertise, and technical assistance. [^bi5auw] [^wx5w41]

## What They Do

ACEEE conducts research, develops policy recommendations, provides technical assistance, and evaluates energy-efficiency programs across buildings, transportation, industry, and the power sector. [^bi5auw] [^wx5w41] Its work is intended to help public agencies, utilities, communities, businesses, and households reduce energy use and associated costs while advancing climate and equity objectives. [^bi5auw] [^wx5w41]

- **Buildings:** Research and policy analysis on building efficiency and energy-saving programs. [^9xrgd7] [^wx5w41]
- **Transportation:** Analysis of fuel-economy standards and transportation energy policy. [^j4ffdw] [^wx5w41]
- **Industry:** Research on industrial innovation and demonstration programs. [^gm5rdw]
- **Power sector:** Analysis of energy efficiency, reliability, and clean-energy policy. [^wx5w41]
- **Behavioral science:** The BEAR Hub applies behavior-informed approaches to increase participation in energy-saving initiatives. [^9xrgd7]
- **State and local technical assistance:** Work supporting energy programs and policies, including analysis of Massachusetts’s Mass Save program. [^bi5auw]
- **Publications and events:** Research reports, policy analysis, webinars, and conferences disseminate findings to practitioners and policymakers. [^gm5rdw] [^9xrgd7]

## Milestones and Signature Output

- **BEAR Hub — 2025:** ACEEE launched the Behavior, Energy, Action and Research Hub to apply behavioral science to real-world energy-saving programs; its first 10 months involved two cities, two agencies, and one business. [^9xrgd7]
- **Mass Save analysis — 2026:** ACEEE published analysis of Massachusetts’s Mass Save programs, reporting that activity from 2011–2024 reduced gas imports by 242.2 million therms in 2024 and generated $38.4 billion in total benefits. [^bi5auw]
- **Industrial innovation research — 2026:** ACEEE published lessons from the U.S. Department of Energy’s Industrial Demonstrations Program, focusing on practices for industrial innovation. [^gm5rdw]
- **Transportation policy analysis — 2026:** ACEEE publicly analyzed fuel-economy standards and argued that weakening them would increase drivers’ costs. [^j4ffdw]
- **Market-transformation research:** ACEEE has developed research on strategies for promoting end-use energy efficiency and market transformation. [^4q042u]

## Impact

- **Impact on society:** ACEEE’s analysis of Mass Save reports that the program generated $38.4 billion in total benefits from 2011 through 2024 and reduced Massachusetts gas imports by 242.2 million therms in 2024. [^bi5auw]
- **Impact on innovation:** ACEEE has helped translate behavioral-science research into operational energy programs through BEAR Hub, which worked with public agencies, cities, and a business to increase participation in energy-saving initiatives. [^9xrgd7]
- **Impact on its industry or domain:** ACEEE connects independent research with policy design and technical assistance across four major energy-consuming sectors, providing a cross-sector model for scaling efficiency policy. [^wx5w41]
- **Historical significance:** ACEEE has operated since 1980 and has maintained a long-running role in U.S. energy-efficiency research and policy development. [^wx5w41]
