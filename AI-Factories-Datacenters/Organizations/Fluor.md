## Value Proposition & Features

Fluor Corporation is a global engineering, procurement, construction and maintenance company serving energy, infrastructure, life sciences, advanced technologies, mining and metals, and government markets. [^E1] Its stated proposition is to deliver “safe, well-executed, capital-efficient projects” through professional and technical expertise. [1]

Core capabilities include:

- **Engineering, procurement and construction:** Fluor integrates design, equipment sourcing, fabrication, construction and commissioning for large capital projects. [1]
- **Maintenance and operations support:** The company provides maintenance services alongside project delivery for industrial and government customers. [^E1]
- **Energy and chemicals:** Fluor supports gas, chemicals, nuclear power and related energy infrastructure, including LNG facilities. [1][3]
- **Infrastructure:** Its transportation work includes major highway and interchange projects, such as the $677 million Oak Hill Parkway project in Texas. [4]
- **Government and national security:** Fluor provides technical solutions for the Department of Energy, NNSA, Department of Defense and Intelligence Community. [6]
- **Advanced industrial and technology facilities:** Fluor works on data centers and other facilities requiring accelerated delivery, power generation and early equipment procurement. [5]
- **Global project delivery:** Fluor operates internationally and has provided engineering, procurement, construction and maintenance services for more than a century. [1]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Fluor is intended for governments, infrastructure owners, energy companies, industrial operators, technology companies and other organizations commissioning complex, capital-intensive facilities or infrastructure. [^E1][1]

It is not intended for individual consumers, small businesses seeking off-the-shelf software, or customers looking for transparent self-service pricing; Fluor delivers bespoke professional and construction services through project contracts.

### Viable Alternatives

- **AECOM:** A large infrastructure and engineering-services alternative for transportation, buildings, water and government projects.
