# Blykalla

## Value Proposition & Features

Blykalla develops **lead-cooled small modular reactors (SMRs)** intended to provide reliable, carbon-free baseload electricity. Its flagship **SEALER** reactor is designed as a compact, scalable power unit for industrial applications and flexible siting. [^E1] [^bcl3iw]

- **Lead-cooled fast-reactor architecture:** SEALER uses liquid lead as coolant; Blykalla says its technology builds on lead-cooled systems deployed since the 1960s. [^bcl3iw]
- **55 MWe output:** The reactor is described in Blykalla’s recruitment materials as a compact 55-megawatt-electric unit. [^oc03c2]
- **Materials innovation:** SEALER relies on a proprietary, patented aluminium-alloyed steel intended to withstand liquid lead’s corrosive effects. [^bcl3iw]
- **Compact deployment:** Blykalla states that SEALER’s small footprint supports rapid deployment and flexible siting. [^bcl3iw]
- **Industrial power supply:** The company positions the reactor for industrial use and dependable electricity generation. [^oc03c2]
- **Scalable production:** Blykalla plans to complete its first reactor by 2030 and begin serial production during the 2030s. [^vtg4yj]
- **Safety-oriented design:** The company markets the technology as a safe and reliable nuclear-power solution. [^E1] [^bcl3iw]

# History and Origin Story

Blykalla was founded in 2013 and is headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden. [^E1] The company’s reactor concept is based on more than 20 years of research and Swedish work on lead-cooled fast-reactor technology. [^oc03c2] Its stated inflection points include developing patented corrosion-resistant materials, advancing the SEALER design, and moving toward a first reactor by 2030. [^bcl3iw] [^vtg4yj]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Blykalla is aimed at industrial customers and other operators seeking compact, reliable, low-carbon baseload electricity from an advanced nuclear plant. [^bcl3iw] [^oc03c2]

It is not currently aimed at ordinary residential electricity consumers or buyers seeking an immediately deployable commercial power product, because the first SEALER reactor is planned for 2030 and serial production for the 2030s. [^vtg4yj]

## Viable Alternatives

- **NuScale Power:** Alternative SMR vendor offering a different light-water reactor architecture.
- **Rolls-Royce SMR:** Alternative modular nuclear system aimed at utility-scale deployment.
- **Terrestrial Energy:** Advanced reactor developer using a molten-salt design rather than lead cooling.
- **Kairos Power:** Advanced reactor developer using a fluoride-salt-cooled technology.
- **Oklo:** Developer of compact advanced reactors for distributed and industrial power applications.

No direct product-comparison source was found in the retrieved material; these are category-level alternatives rather than confirmed Blykalla customer substitutes.
