# US Green Building Council

- _USGBC is a nonprofit standards and advocacy organization that makes sustainable building more measurable and actionable through LEED._
- The exact legal form, founding year, headquarters, and organizational footprint were not established by the available search results. USGBC’s core work is associated with LEED and with convening professionals around sustainable buildings and communities. A consultant tracks it because its certification framework can influence building design, construction, operations, procurement, and policy.

## Mission and Identity

> “USGBC is committed to a sustainable, prosperous future through LEED.” [^2mzee5]

USGBC says its mission is to “transform the way buildings and communities are designed, built and operated,” with the goal of enabling an environmentally and socially responsible environment. Its public agenda emphasizes LEED, green-building education, industry convening, and policy engagement; its 2026 activity included participation in California clean-energy legislation and Climate Week programming. [^2mzee5] [^f9tmdp]

- **Stated principles:** Sustainability, prosperity, environmental responsibility, and social responsibility are the central principles visible in the supplied organizational description.

## What They Do

USGBC develops and promotes LEED-related systems, convenes the building sector, supports education and professional participation, and engages in public-policy discussions concerning sustainable buildings and clean energy. Its ecosystem includes project teams and accounts used in connection with USGBC/GBCI LEED projects. [^ovwe6q]

- **LEED:** Certification framework and associated project activity for sustainable buildings and communities. [^ovwe6q]
- **Education and professional engagement:** Public programming and participation opportunities connected with green-building practice. [^p03x9l]
- **[[Greenbuild International Conference & Expo]]:** Industry conference programming, including the 2026 event in New York City. [^4ll8a4]
- **[[Sources/Events/Green Schools Conference]]:** Conference programming for sustainable schools; the 2027 event was announced for Austin. [^6zyjn9]
- **Women in Green:** Annual event and scholarship activity supporting women in the green-building field. [^p03x9l]
- **Policy advocacy:** Coalition and legislative engagement on data centers, grid reliability, clean energy, and building-certification policy. [^2mzee5]

## History and Origin Story

The available results associate USGBC’s formation with 1993 and identify Charlie Cichetti, Mike Italiano, and Rick Fedrizzi as co-founders, but this information came from a podcast listing rather than a primary organizational history. [^1g52ay] The organization’s later public identity is strongly tied to LEED and to industry convening through events such as Greenbuild. [^4ll8a4]

- **1990–1991:** Charlie Cichetti is described as having drafted an initial green-building standard through an ASTM subcommittee; the account says its categories later became the basis for LEED. [^1g52ay]
- **1993:** USGBC is described as having been co-founded by Charlie Cichetti, Mike Italiano, and Rick Fedrizzi. [^1g52ay]
- **2012:** JPMorgan Chase’s 270 Park Avenue was described by USGBC as the world’s largest LEED Platinum project under LEED v2009 Operations and Maintenance at the time of certification. [^4ll8a4]
- **2026:** USGBC continued policy advocacy, professional programming, and major-event activity, including California legislative engagement, Climate Week, Greenbuild, and Women in Green. [^2mzee5] [^f9tmdp] [^p03x9l] [^4ll8a4]

## Milestones and Signature Output

- **LEED certification:** USGBC’s defining standards and market instrument for evaluating sustainable buildings and communities. [^ovwe6q]
- **Greenbuild:** USGBC-associated industry convening, with the 2026 conference positioned around major building-sector projects and sustainability practice. [^4ll8a4]
- **Green Schools Conference:** A dedicated conference program focused on sustainable schools, with the 2027 event scheduled for Austin. [^6zyjn9]
- **Women in Green:** A recurring event with scholarship applications and a 2026 luncheon. [^p03x9l]
- **California policy engagement:** USGBC joined a coalition opposing California AB 1704 and commented on SB 1398’s alternative pathway for adoption of a green-certification program. [^2mzee5]
