Submer is an IT services and IT consulting company operating across AI-infrastructure layers including power, cooling, data centers, and compute. It is part of Submer Group, which combines specialist companies to deploy and scale AI workloads using liquid-cooled infrastructure and modular data-center platforms. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

Submer Group positions itself as a full-stack AI-infrastructure provider addressing power availability, thermal density, deployment speed, and data sovereignty. Its platform approach combines liquid cooling, modular data centers, physical infrastructure development, and GPU services rather than selling cooling equipment alone. [^6d5eqz] [^f8xa57] [^iziev2]

Core features include industrialized liquid-cooled infrastructure, modular data-center platforms based on NVIDIA reference designs, integrated power, cooling, networking, and IT systems, and GPU infrastructure-as-a-service for inference and cloud gaming. [^6d5eqz] [^f8xa57]

- **Liquid-cooled infrastructure** for high-density AI workloads; the group reports more than 500 MW deployed. [^6d5eqz] [^iziev2]
- **Modular data-center platforms** factory-built and tested before on-site commissioning. [^6d5eqz]
- **Integrated infrastructure** combining IT, power, cooling, and networking as one system. [^6d5eqz]
- **GPU-as-a-service** through InferX, covering sovereign GPU and enterprise AI services. [^f8xa57]
- **AI and cloud services at the edge** through Radian Arc’s InferX and RadianX product families. [^f8xa57]
- **Data-center development and operations** through Rubix Data Centers. [^iziev2]
- **Powered-land access**, with the group reporting more than 8 GW across the Americas, EMEA, and APAC. [^6d5eqz] [^iziev2]

The architecture spans from site-level land and power through cooling, modular deployment, and compute or cloud services. This makes Submer relevant to [[concepts/Explainers for AI/High-Density Compute|High-Density Compute]] deployments where conventional air cooling and bespoke construction can constrain rack density or deployment schedules. [^6d5eqz] [^iziev2]

# History and Origin Story

Submer was founded in 2015 and is headquartered in Rubí, Barcelona, Spain. [^E1] The company has since expanded from liquid-cooling engineering into a group structure spanning AI infrastructure, modular data centers, physical data-center development, and GPU services; the group describes its operating companies as addressing the full infrastructure stack from land and power to cloud and edge. [^f8xa57] [^iziev2]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Submer Group is aimed at neoclouds, hyperscalers, AI operators, telecom operators, and organizations requiring high-density AI infrastructure, modular deployment, sovereign GPU capacity, or large-scale data-center development. [^6d5eqz] [^f8xa57] [^iziev2]

It is not a natural fit for small organizations seeking ordinary enterprise hosting, low-density workloads, or an off-the-shelf desktop or single-server cooling product; the available descriptions focus on industrial-scale infrastructure and custom-engineered deployments. [^6d5eqz] [^f8xa57] [^iziev2]
