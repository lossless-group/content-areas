## Value Proposition & Features

Bild is a **cloud-native [[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Concepts/PDM-PLM Platforms|PDM]]/PLM platform** for mechanical and multidisciplinary hardware teams, focused on version control, BOM management, supplier collaboration, and engineering change workflows. Its positioning emphasizes replacing server-, vault-, database-, and VPN-dependent legacy systems with browser- and desktop-accessible tooling.[1] [^E1]

Core capabilities include CAD file and revision management, item and BOM control, engineering change orders, metadata governance, design reviews, supplier access, and automated file generation. Bild supports workflows across SolidWorks, Creo, and other engineering systems.[^E1]

- **CAD version control and revision history**.[^E1]
- **BOM management** with synchronization between assembly structures and BOMs.[1]
- **Engineering change orders** routed to named reviewers.[1]
- **Supplier collaboration** through scoped access to relevant drawings and data.[1]
- **Browser and desktop workflows** for engineers.[1]
- **Integrated 3D viewing** and access for users with different permissions.[2]
- **Cloud-native architecture** without a vault server, SQL database, or VPN.[1]
- **AI-driven engineering workflow automation**.[^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Bild is aimed at mechanical and multidisciplinary hardware teams that need centralized CAD, BOM, revision, ECO, supplier, and design-review workflows across product development.[^E1] The Corvus case study describes use by engineers, viewers, downloaders, BOM readers, and approvers, indicating support for cross-functional access.[2]

It is not a strong fit for organizations seeking only general-purpose file storage or teams without structured hardware-development workflows. It may also be unsuitable for buyers requiring a legacy PLM deployment model, although that limitation is an inference from Bild’s cloud-native positioning.[1] [^E1]

## Viable Alternatives

- **SOLIDWORKS PDM:** A direct alternative for teams centered on SOLIDWORKS file and revision management; Bild explicitly positions itself as a cloud-native alternative.[1]
- **Dassault Systèmes 3DEXPERIENCE:** A broader enterprise platform and the system Corvus reportedly replaced with Bild.[2]
- **Traditional PLM platforms:** Relevant for organizations requiring broader enterprise lifecycle controls, though Bild’s materials contrast their typical implementation timelines with Bild’s stated 3–10-day go-live.[1]
- **Other cloud-native PDM/PLM tools:** Potential alternatives for buyers prioritizing browser access, reduced infrastructure, and faster deployment; no additional exact competitors were identified in the available search results.
