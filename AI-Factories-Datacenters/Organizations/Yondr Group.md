# Yondr Group

## Retrieved sources

Yondr Group develops, owns, and operates hyperscale data centers and provides complex data-center capacity solutions for large technology companies. Its stated mission is “global capacity responsible delivery.” [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

Yondr Group serves hyperscale technology companies that need large-scale, rapidly deployable data-center capacity. Its proposition combines development, ownership, and operations rather than supplying only construction or colocation services. [^E1]

Core features, in priority order:

- Hyperscale data-center development for large technology customers. [^E1]
- Long-term ownership and operation of digital-infrastructure assets. [^E1]
- Complex capacity solutions tailored to customer requirements. [^E1]
- Scalable infrastructure for cloud and advanced data-processing workloads. [^E1]
- Global delivery model focused on stability, clarity, and speed. [^E1]
- Standardized operational reporting, including centralized live HSE dashboards and a “single source of truth” for performance data. [^0jgiw1]
- More than 420 MW of capacity committed to hyperscale customers, with potential capacity exceeding 1 GW, according to a 2026 transaction report. [^68zz7k]

Yondr’s assets fit the broader category of [[Vocabulary/Data Centers|Data Centers]], with campuses developed across multiple markets and operated as institutional digital infrastructure. [^E1] [^68zz7k]

## Product Roadmap / Announcements

As of October 10, 2026,

- **August 3, 2026:** Yondr and Cerberus acquired a 40-acre site in Manassas, Virginia, for a planned 72 MW hyperscale data-center campus expected to enter service in 2029. [^e7ntoy]
- **July 31, 2026:** Yondr announced an agreement to sell a majority stake in its Slough campus to GLIL Infrastructure while retaining a minority stake and continuing to operate the campus. [^l3f9cs]
- **September 25, 2026:** Yondr reported that its HSE team had replaced fragmented manual reporting with centralized live dashboards. [^0jgiw1]

## Recent Developments

- **October 10, 2026:** Cathexis reportedly agreed to sell Yondr to DigitalBridge and La Caisse for $5.8 billion; the report described the transaction as an exit for Cathexis, which founded Yondr in 2018. [^68zz7k]
- **October 9, 2026:** Data Center Frontier reported that Yondr had developed a completed 98 MW data center in Johor, Malaysia, later acquired by Vantage Data Centers. [^za1jn8]
- **September 2026:** Yondr’s reported development pipeline included a 72 MW Northern Virginia campus and a 550 MW planned Lancaster, Texas, campus. [^e7ntoy] [^wo90nb]

# History and Origin Story

Yondr was founded in 2018 by Cathexis and developed as a hyperscale data-center developer, owner, and operator. [^68zz7k] The company later expanded its committed and potential capacity across multiple markets, including projects in Malaysia, Europe, and the United States. [^68zz7k] [^za1jn8] In 2026, reports described a $5.8 billion sale to DigitalBridge and La Caisse, marking a major ownership transition. [^68zz7k]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Yondr is designed for hyperscale technology companies and other large customers requiring substantial, scalable data-center capacity and operational support. [^E1]

It is not positioned as a consumer cloud product, small-business colocation service, or self-service infrastructure tool; its offering is based on complex, bespoke capacity solutions and large physical campuses. [^E1]
