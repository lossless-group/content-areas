# The Climate Group

![The Climate Group logo or global climate-action network image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3f/Climate_Group_logo.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

- _The Climate Group is an international nonprofit that builds influential business and government coalitions to accelerate the transition to a net-zero economy._
- The organization describes itself as an international nonprofit founded in 2004, with offices in London, Amsterdam, Beijing, New Delhi, and New York.[^uvtt7b] Its work focuses on high-emitting systems, including renewable electricity, transport, industry, and methane reduction.[^uvtt7b] Consultants track it because its coalitions convert corporate and subnational-government commitments into coordinated policy and market action.[^uvtt7b]

## Mission and Identity

> “Our goal is a world of net zero carbon emissions by 2050, with greater prosperity for all.”[^uvtt7b]

Climate Group says it focuses on systems with the highest emissions and on areas where its networks can drive change.[^uvtt7b] It positions itself as a coalition-builder that creates large networks, holds organizations accountable, turns commitments into action, and shares results to encourage wider adoption.[^uvtt7b]

- **Operating principle:** Build influential networks and hold organizations accountable for translating climate commitments into measurable action.[^uvtt7b]

## What They Do

Climate Group operates voluntary and government-facing coalitions that coordinate corporate procurement, fleet electrification, subnational climate policy, methane reduction, and industrial decarbonization.[^uvtt7b][^eija7q][^ot56tk][^ok42of] Its value comes from convening organizations, publishing comparative data, advocating for policy, and making collective commitments visible to wider markets.[^uvtt7b]

- **RE100:** A corporate network focused on achieving 100% renewable electricity.[^uvtt7b]
- **EV100:** A corporate network promoting zero-emission road transport and electric fleets.[^ot56tk]
- **Under2 Coalition:** A subnational-government climate network referenced through Climate Group’s broader coalition work.[^uvtt7b]
- **Climate Week NYC:** A major convening platform bringing businesses, governments, and civil society together; the 2026 event featured more than 1,500 events.[^mjxlr8]
- **Methane action:** Climate Group participates in the Subnational Methane Action Coalition, which targets methane from sectors including waste and agriculture.[^eija7q]
- **Industrial decarbonization:** Climate Group joined the Blend Better Cement campaign, which promotes lower-carbon blended cement through government and industry action.[^ok42of]

## History and Origin Story

Climate Group was founded in 2004 as an international nonprofit focused on accelerating climate action through networks of businesses and governments.[^uvtt7b] Its subsequent model has centered on high-emitting systems and coalition platforms such as RE100, EV100, Climate Week NYC, and subnational-government initiatives.[^uvtt7b][^mjxlr8][^ot56tk]

- **2004** — Climate Group was founded as an international nonprofit.[^uvtt7b]
- **2023** — The Subnational Methane Action Coalition was founded by the California state government at COP28, with Climate Group participating in subsequent coalition activity.[^eija7q]
- **2026** — Climate Week NYC expanded to more than 1,500 events, according to Climate Group.[^mjxlr8]
- **2026** — RE100 reported 408 member companies and collective renewable-electricity use of 59%.[^uvtt7b]

## Impact

- **Impact on society**
  - Climate Group’s RE100 network has created a shared reporting and accountability mechanism for hundreds of companies pursuing renewable electricity; the latest report covered 408 members.[^uvtt7b]
  - Climate Week NYC has become a large-scale public and institutional convening platform, with more than 1,500 events reported in 2026.[^mjxlr8]

- **Impact on innovation**
  - Climate Group has helped institutionalize network-based climate accountability, using common commitments, disclosure, and peer pressure to accelerate corporate action on electricity and transport.[^uvtt7b][^ot56tk]
  - Its coalition model links voluntary corporate action with public-policy advocacy, as shown by EV100’s intervention on European truck standards.[^ot56tk]

- **Impact on its industry or domain**
  - RE100 has established a widely used corporate framework for tracking progress toward 100% renewable electricity, with annual disclosure data spanning hundreds of companies.[^uvtt7b]
  - Climate Group’s campaigns increasingly connect corporate demand with industrial-policy interventions, including blended cement and heavy-duty-vehicle electrification.[^ot56tk][^ok42of]

- **Historical significance**
  - Founded in 2004, Climate Group belongs to the generation of international climate nonprofits that shifted part of climate governance toward cross-border networks of companies and subnational governments.[^uvtt7b]
  - Its platforms illustrate the growing role of voluntary coalitions in shaping renewable-energy procurement, transport electrification, and sectoral decarbonization.[^uvtt7b][^ot56tk]
