# Virtana

## Value Proposition & Features

Virtana is a hybrid and multi-cloud observability platform that correlates telemetry, topology, events, and dependencies across applications, services, Kubernetes, infrastructure, networks, storage, cloud resources, data pipelines, and AI workloads. [^4xqfw3] Its stated objective is to help teams distinguish symptoms from constraints, identify evidence-backed root cause, stabilize service levels, control costs, and automate governed remediation. [^4xqfw3]

- **[[System Dependency Graph]]:** Continuously maps relationships across applications, services, Kubernetes, infrastructure, networks, storage, cloud resources, data pipelines, and AI workloads. [^4xqfw3]
- **[[Full-Stack Telemetry]]:** Collects and correlates metrics, logs, traces, events, topology, and infrastructure signals across operational domains. [^4xqfw3] [^2b2fp5]
- **Event Intelligence:** Correlates deployment events, configuration changes, and operational behavior to separate symptoms from underlying constraints. [^4xqfw3]
- **[[concepts/Explainers for AI/Diagnostic AI Agents]]:** Investigate incidents and identify evidence-backed root cause using system-wide dependencies and live operational evidence. [^4xqfw3] [^ykjuy9]
- **[[Remediation Agents]]:** Recommend or execute governed corrective actions through policies and workflows. [^4xqfw3]
- **[[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Concepts/AI Factories|AI-Factory]] observability:** Connects [[Vocabulary/Graphics Processing Units|GPU]] utilization, health, power, and cost to workloads, applications, data movement, storage, networks, Kubernetes, training, and inference. [^ykjuy9]
- **Hybrid deployment:** Supports SaaS and self-hosted deployment, including customer-controlled environments. [^2djaf6]
- **Agent and API access:** Provides natural-language troubleshooting, policy authoring, documentation assistance, and a metrics service for retrieving time-series data. [^2b2fp5] [^5dzbp7]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Virtana is designed for enterprise IT, platform, DevOps, infrastructure, cloud, Kubernetes, data-pipeline, and AI-infrastructure teams that need correlated visibility across hybrid or multi-cloud environments. [^4xqfw3] [^2djaf6] [^ykjuy9] The supplied metadata says the company is trusted by more than 150 Global 2000 organizations. [^E1]

It is not a natural fit for small teams seeking a simple single-application monitor, a low-cost self-service product with published prices, or an observability deployment limited to one infrastructure layer; Virtana’s documented positioning centers on broad system context, enterprise scale, and custom deployment complexity. [^4xqfw3] [^2djaf6]

## Viable Alternatives

- **[[Tooling/Data Utilities/DataDog|DataDog]]:** A broad cloud monitoring and observability alternative for teams seeking integrated infrastructure, application, and service monitoring.
- **[[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Organizations/Dynatrace]]:** An enterprise observability alternative emphasizing automated dependency analysis and application intelligence.
- **[[New Relic]]:** A developer-oriented observability alternative covering application performance, infrastructure, logs, and user experience.
