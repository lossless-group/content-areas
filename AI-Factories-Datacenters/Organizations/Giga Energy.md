# Giga Energy

## Retrieved sources

The provided source identifies Giga Energy as a manufacturer, developer, and operator of modular AI data centers and energy infrastructure, offering turnkey delivery from design through energization. It also reports a 2019 founding year, Houston headquarters, 233 employees, and total funding of $13.4 million. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

Giga Energy positions itself as a full-stack infrastructure partner for AI projects, combining powered land, electrical equipment, modular data-center systems, and development services. Its stated approach is to reduce deployment time by factory-building and pre-testing major infrastructure before shipment. [^E1] [^hio60m]

- **Turnkey AI data centers:** Giga Energy develops modular [[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Concepts/AI Factories|AI-Factory]] [[Vocabulary/Data Centers|Data Centers]] infrastructure from design through energization. [^E1]
- **GigaPod compute modules:** Factory-built modules support rapid deployment of AI compute capacity. [^E1]
- **Integrated power systems:** Its prefabricated system combines power modules, UPS e-houses, and compute modules. [^hio60m]
- **Manufactured electrical equipment:** Giga Energy builds UL 891 switchboards rated up to 5,000A at 480V in Long Beach, California. [^hio60m]
- **On-site generation:** Its modular diesel generator is standby-rated at 3.3 MW and is built and tested before shipment. [^83223c]
- **Modular deployment:** The company claims that pre-integrated systems reduce on-site MEP construction labor by 10× compared with traditional field builds. [^hio60m]
- **Powered-land development:** Giga Energy develops and operates sites intended for high-density AI infrastructure. [^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Giga Energy is aimed at AI infrastructure developers, data-center operators, GPU-compute providers, and organizations needing high-density compute capacity with integrated power and cooling. Its offerings are designed for projects requiring modular deployment, factory-built equipment, and access to powered sites. [^E1] [^hio60m]

It is not primarily positioned as a general-purpose cloud provider, a consumer electricity retailer, or a software-only data-center-management company.
