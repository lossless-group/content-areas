# AI Factories & Datacenters

Market research content area covering AI factories, hyperscale and neocloud datacenters, and the stack that feeds them: compute and accelerators, power and grid interconnects, cooling, networking, real estate and siting, financing, and the operators building it all.

| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `Organizations/` | Companies, operators, developers, investors, agencies |
| `Concepts/` | Explainers (e.g. AI factory, liquid cooling, behind-the-meter power) |
| `Topics/` | Ongoing themes and initiatives |
| `Issues/` | Problems and constraints (power scarcity, water use, permitting) |
| `Vocabulary/` | Short definitions of industry terms |
| `Sources/` | Reports, datasets, primary-source PDFs |
