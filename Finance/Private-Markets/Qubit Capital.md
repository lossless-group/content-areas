## Value Proposition & Features

Qubit Capital is an AI-powered fundraising and investor-matchmaking platform for startups, founded in 2020 and headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. [^E1] It helps founders identify suitable investors and conduct targeted outreach. [^E1]

Core features:

- **Investor discovery and mapping:** Identifies and maps potentially relevant investors. [^E1]
- **Investor matchmaking:** Uses AI-powered matching to connect startups with investors whose profiles fit their fundraising needs. [^E1] This aligns with the broader concept of [[concepts/Explainers for AI/AI Matchmaking|AI Matchmaking]].
- **Targeted outreach:** Supports more focused investor communications rather than broad, undifferentiated outreach. [^E1]
- **Data-room creation:** Helps startups prepare a structured data room for investor review. [^E1]
- **Fundraising strategy:** Provides advanced fundraising strategies tailored to a startup’s stage and industry. [^E1]
- **Stage-specific support:** Offers fundraising solutions across startup stages. [^E1]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Qubit Capital is aimed at startups seeking investor discovery, targeted fundraising outreach, data-room support, and fundraising strategy across different stages and industries. [^E1]

It is not clearly positioned for retail investors, public-market trading, or founders seeking a fully disclosed self-service pricing model; no reliable source confirms those use cases.

### Viable Alternatives

- **Gust:** Startup fundraising and investor-networking platform; a potential alternative for founders seeking structured fundraising support.
- **DocSend:** Data-room and investor-document sharing platform; relevant for the diligence and data-room portion of Qubit Capital’s offering.
- **Affinity:** Relationship-intelligence platform; relevant for investor relationship tracking and network analysis.
- **Visible:** Fundraising and investor-reporting software; relevant for startup-investor communications.
- **OpenVC:** Investor-discovery platform; relevant for founders seeking investor matching and outreach.
