[[The New VC Stack]]

Cake Equity is a cap-table and equity-management platform for growing companies, covering ownership modeling, 409A valuations, stock options, compliance, workflows, and stakeholder access. Its materials position it especially for startups preparing for Seed and Series A fundraising. [^ehs3og] [^3ef4bj]

## Value Proposition & Features

Cake describes its platform as a way to keep cap-table data, signed paperwork, transaction history, and stakeholder access in one place, reducing diligence and reporting work. [^ehs3og] It also provides employee and investor portals so stakeholders can view holdings, vesting progress, and documents directly. [^3ef4bj]

- Cap-table management and ownership modeling. [^ehs3og]
- SAFE, note, shareholder, and equity-grant tracking. [^ehs3og]
- 409A valuation workflows connected to company data. [^m1d69a]
- Stock-option and equity-incentive-plan administration. [^ehs3og] [^m1d69a]
- Compliance and audit-ready reporting. [^ehs3og]
- Investor and employee portals with document access. [^ehs3og] [^3ef4bj]
- Permission controls with four team-access levels. [^ehs3og]
- Managed migrations from other cap-table platforms, including Pulley, at no extra migration cost. [^3ef4bj]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Cake is aimed at growing startups managing ownership from initial SAFEs through more complex financing rounds, particularly companies approaching or completing Seed and Series A fundraising. [^ehs3og] [^3ef4bj] It is also designed for teams that need investor, employee, legal, and finance stakeholders to access structured equity records. [^ehs3og]

It is less suited to organizations seeking only a basic spreadsheet replacement without valuation, compliance, document, or stakeholder-portal needs. The retrieved material does not establish its suitability for large public companies or highly specialized multinational equity programs.

## Viable Alternatives

- **Pulley:** A directly relevant alternative for startup cap-table and equity management; Cake specifically documents migration workflows from Pulley. [^3ef4bj]
- **Carta:** A major alternative in cap-table, equity-compensation, and private-company administration; no detailed comparison was available in the retrieved results.
- **Spreadsheet-based administration:** Suitable only for very small or simple ownership structures, but lacks Cake’s integrated documents, permissions, portals, and managed workflows. [^ehs3og]
- **Law-firm or finance-provider administration:** May suit companies outsourcing equity operations, although the retrieved sources do not provide a direct product comparison.
