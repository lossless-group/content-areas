> **Question:**
> Describe the company Symbrosia and what they do that benefits their customers.
> 

Symbrosia is a Hawaii-based company that develops and produces **natural seaweed feed additives—specifically using the red seaweed Asparagopsis taxiformis—to drastically reduce methane emissions from livestock**. [^1qy8bo] [^7mc8hx] [^4axoi3] [^j7yql8] Their flagship product, **SeaGraze™**, can lower methane emissions from cattle by over 80%, addressing a major challenge in agricultural greenhouse gas reduction. [^7mc8hx] [^j7yql8]

A conceptual diagram could illustrate how the Asparagopsis-based feed additive interrupts methane production in a cow’s rumen, thereby reducing the amount released when the animal burps.

**How Symbrosia Benefits Their Customers:**
- **Livestock producers—ranchers and dairy farmers—use SeaGraze™ as a nutritional supplement.** When added to feed, this product leads to a significant reduction (70–90%) in methane emissions from cattle, helping producers respond to increasing regulatory and consumer demand for sustainable practices. [^1qy8bo] [^7mc8hx] [^j7yql8]
- **Premium market positioning:** By enabling the “world’s most sustainable animal products,” Symbrosia’s innovation allows producers to command higher prices and differentiate their beef and dairy as climate-friendly. [^1qy8bo]
- **Support for carbon reduction goals:** For companies and brands with climate targets (such as Danone), SeaGraze™ offers a practical tool for emissions compliance and brand reputation improvement. [^7mc8hx]
- **Co-benefits to animal health:** SeaGraze™ is described not just as an emissions solution but also as a mineral-rich supplement, potentially benefiting animal nutrition. [^j7yql8]

**![Practical example or use case visualization](https://img.chemie.de/Portal/Organization/173853_n1e_skGpz.png?tr=n-xl10)**
An illustration or infographic could depict cattle at a farm that uses SeaGraze™, with emissions-reduction statistics and certification badges for low-methane products.

**Additional Details and Innovations:**
- **Technology leadership:** Symbrosia has developed proprietary strains of Asparagopsis taxiformis that are more productive and resilient, which improves large-scale cultivation efficiency and costs. [^7mc8hx]
- **Sustainability in operations:** The company uses 100% renewable energy, does not require freshwater input, and relies mostly on seawater, making their production process environmentally friendly. [^j7yql8]
- **Industry partnerships and scaling:** Symbrosia is collaborating with researchers, farmer networks, distributors, and brands to expand supply chains and maximize adoption of low-methane animal products. [^1qy8bo] [^7mc8hx]
- **Recognition and investment:** The company has received accolades such as the Blue Climate Initiative’s Ocean Innovation Prize and garnered significant investment from notable partners like Danone Manifesto Ventures. [^7mc8hx]
